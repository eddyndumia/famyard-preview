1e:Tace,<p><strong>CHAKA, NYERI COUNTY</strong> — Representatives from NCBA and Family Bank joined Famyard Enterprises Ltd at its Chaka plots launch, a visible show of the financing relationships the company has been building to make land purchases easier for buyers.</p>
<h2>Banking on Land</h2>
<p>Both banks had branded tents and staff on site throughout the event, speaking with prospective buyers about financing options alongside Famyard’s own sales team. It’s a pairing that has become more common at Kenyan land launches generally, as developers look to banks to close the gap between a buyer’s interest and their ability to pay for a plot outright.</p>
<p>For Famyard, the partnerships are also a trust signal. A company whose plots are financeable through established banks has, implicitly, passed a level of due diligence those banks require before backing a purchase — documentation, title status, and site verification among them.</p>
<h2>What It Means for Buyers</h2>
<p>For buyers who might otherwise need years to save for a plot outright, financing partners like NCBA and Family Bank shorten that timeline. It’s a similar calculation to the one Famyard walks through in its guide for <a href="/famyard-preview/blog/land-investment-for-retirees-what-changes-after-50">land investment for retirees</a>, where cash flow and financing options matter as much as the plot itself.</p>
<p>The financing conversations at the Chaka launch centred on the same plots at the heart of the day’s other ceremonies — <a href="/famyard-preview/properties/chaka-lane-15-famyard-estate-50x50-plots">Chaka Lane 15 Famyard Estate</a> and <a href="/famyard-preview/properties/chaka-ranch-homes-premium-fully-serviced-1-4-acre-plots-for-sale-in-chaka-nyeri-2">Chaka Ranch Homes</a>, both fully serviced with graded roads, water, and electricity already installed.</p>
<h2>A Recurring Presence</h2>
<p>Bank partners were a constant thread through the day, appearing in the handshake photos that followed the land blessing, the tree-planting drive, and the title deed handovers covered elsewhere in this series. Their presence at each stage of the event, rather than a single branded booth off to the side, was as much the story as the ceremonies themselves.</p>
