<p><strong>The Title Processing Journey Unveiled</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Initial Verification:</strong> The journey begins with verifying the authenticity of the property’s title and ensuring that it’s free from encumbrances or disputes.</li>
<li><strong>Land Control Board Approval:</strong> This step involves seeking approval from the relevant Land Control Board, demonstrating that the transaction complies with the local land regulations.</li>
<li><strong>Land Survey:</strong> A land survey is conducted to ascertain the accurate boundaries and dimensions of the property, ensuring that it aligns with the title’s description.</li>
<li><strong>Valuation and Assessment:</strong> The property’s value is assessed, which is a critical aspect for determining taxes, fees, and the property’s market worth.</li>
<li><strong>Stamp Duty Payment:</strong> Stamp duty, a tax imposed on property transactions, must be paid based on the property’s value.</li>
<li><strong>Application for Consent:</strong> If the property is categorized as agricultural or controlled property, consent must be obtained from the relevant authorities.</li>
<li><strong>Land Rent Clearance:</strong> Clearance from the relevant county government for any outstanding land rent payments is obtained.</li>
<li><strong>Land Control Board Confirmation:</strong> The Land Control Board confirms that the property’s transaction adheres to legal requirements.</li>
<li><strong>Application for Title Deed:</strong> The application for a new title deed or transfer is submitted to the relevant land office, along with the necessary documents and fees.</li>
<li><strong>Processing and Issuance:</strong> The title deed is processed, verified, and finally issued, marking the legal transfer of ownership.</li>
</ol>
