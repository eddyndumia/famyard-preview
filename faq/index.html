Frequently asked questions about buying land

Why We Are The Best

General questions

What is Famyard Enterprises Ltd?

Famyard Enterprises Ltd is a premier real estate company based in Nyeri and Jikaze, Kenya. We specialize in selling and managing land with fast title deed processing, transparent pricing (including title deed costs), and tailored services for both local and international clients.

What types of land properties do you offer?

We offer a diverse range of land parcels ideal for residential and commercial investment. Our offerings are strategically located in Nyeri, Jikaze, and other key areas, making them attractive for the middle class, upper class, and the Kenyan diaspora.

How does Famyard Enterprises stand out from competitors?

Our competitive edge lies in our 10-year legacy, rapid title deed processing, all-inclusive pricing, exceptional customer service, and comprehensive bank financing options. We also provide personalized site visits and property management, ensuring every client’s needs are met with transparency and efficiency.

How quickly can title deeds be processed?

We pride ourselves on efficiency. Our streamlined procedures ensure that title deeds are processed swiftly, giving you peace of mind and allowing you to secure your investment without unnecessary delays.

How We Work

Other questions

Do you offer site visits?

Yes, we offer detailed site visits to help you assess the location, potential, and unique features of each property. Our experienced team will guide you through every step during your visit.

What financing options are available?

We partner with reputable banks to provide flexible financing options. Our goal is to make land ownership accessible, whether you are part of the middle class, an upscale buyer, or an international investor.

How do I begin the process of buying land with Famyard Enterprises?

Simply contact our customer service team by phone, email, or through our website. We’ll guide you through property selection, financing, and the documentation process to ensure a smooth transaction.

Can international clients buy land through Famyard Enterprises?

Absolutely. We cater to clients worldwide, offering multilingual support, tailored financing, and guidance through every step of the purchase process, ensuring a seamless experience for international investors.

What legal safeguards do you have in place for land transactions?

Our transactions adhere strictly to Kenyan property laws. We work with experienced legal professionals to ensure that every deal is secure, transparent, and compliant with local regulations.

What documentation is needed to purchase land?

Generally, you will need valid identification, proof of funds, and, if applicable, financing documents. Our team will provide you with a detailed checklist to ensure a smooth transaction process.

How do I schedule a site visit or consultation?

You can easily schedule a site visit or consultation by contacting our friendly customer service team via phone, email, or our website. We’ll coordinate a visit at a time that suits you best.

How can I stay updated on new listings and special offers?

Stay informed by subscribing to our newsletter, following us on social media, or visiting our website regularly. We update our listings and special financing deals frequently to ensure you never miss an opportunity.
