Our Services

Empowering your land investment journey

01

Buying and Selling of Land and Plots

We specialize in facilitating seamless land transactions, ensuring our clients get genuine, legally verified plots. Whether you're looking to invest, develop, or settle, we guide you through the entire buying or selling process with transparency.

02

Surveyor Services

Accurate land mapping and boundary identification are essential for ownership clarity. Our licensed surveyors provide professional services, including subdivision, title deed processing, and boundary realignment, ensuring compliance with local land regulations.

03

Conveyancing Services

We handle all legal aspects of property ownership transfer, ensuring that buyers and sellers complete transactions smoothly.

Land Searches and Control Boards

Before purchasing any land, we conduct thorough searches to confirm ownership, legitimacy, and any encumbrances. We also assist in obtaining control board approvals required for land transactions, giving our clients peace of mind.

Succession

We provide expert guidance in transferring land ownership after the passing of a titleholder. Our team helps families navigate the legal process of succession, ensuring rightful inheritance and smooth title transfers.

Transforming Communities Through Value-Added Properties

At Famyard Enterprises Ltd, we specialize in developing value-added plots that are ready for immediate residential or commercial use. Our projects are strategically located in high-growth areas, offering our clients not just land, but opportunities to be part of thriving communities.

Commitment to Transparency and Customer Satisfaction

We prioritize honesty and clarity in all our dealings, ensuring that our clients are well-informed at every step of their investment journey. Our dedicated customer service team is always ready to address inquiries, reflecting our commitment to building lasting relationships based on trust.

Empowering Investors with Secure and Affordable Options

Understanding the challenges of land ownership, we offer affordable plots with flexible payment plans, making it easier for Kenyans to invest in real estate. Our rigorous due diligence ensures that all properties have ready title deeds, providing peace of mind to our investors.
