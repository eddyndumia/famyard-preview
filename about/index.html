Our Journey

Ten years of building trust

  1. 2015

    Establishing a Strong Foundation

    Famyard Enterprises Ltd officially launches in Nyeri, laying the groundwork for a modern approach to Kenyan land investments.

  2. 2016

    Introducing Diaspora Outreach

    Recognizing the growing interest among Kenyans abroad, Famyard begins offering specialized services for diaspora clients. This initiative simplifies the property acquisition process for those living overseas, broadening the company’s client base globally.

  3. 2017

    First 100 Transactions Completed

    In just two years, Famyard celebrates completing over 100 successful land transactions. Clients commend the firm’s speed in finalizing title deeds and the consistent, open communication that builds trust and fosters repeat business.

  4. 2018

    Building Strategic Relationships

    Famyard forms strategic alliances with local financial institutions to provide clients with flexible payment options and tailored financing solutions. These partnerships further solidify the company’s commitment to making land ownership accessible.

  5. 2019

    Expanding Digital Presence

    The company invests in digital marketing and online platforms, making it easier for clients to browse land listings, schedule site visits, and engage with the Famyard team. This digital shift accommodates a growing audience across Kenya and beyond.

  6. 2020

    Innovating Through Remote Services

    In response to changing market needs, Famyard introduces remote consultations and online site visits, allowing clients to evaluate properties from anywhere. This innovation ensures minimal disruptions for both local and international investors.

  7. 2021

    Reaching New Frontiers

    The firm extends its reach to additional counties, introducing new projects in emerging growth areas. Continued focus on efficiency and transparency leads to a notable increase in satisfied clients, further elevating Famyard’s reputation.

  8. 2022

    Strengthening Diaspora Partnerships

    Leveraging positive feedback from diaspora investors, Famyard enhances its international offerings. More tailored financing packages and streamlined processes help Kenyans abroad invest confidently, reinforcing the company’s global appeal.

  9. 2023

    Commitment to Excellence

    Famyard continues refining its services by embracing new technologies and deepening relationships with financial partners. Ongoing improvements in customer experience and strategic growth initiatives highlight the company’s unwavering commitment to modern, trustworthy land investments.

  10. 2024

    Ongoing Innovations & Strategic Growth

    Famyard Enterprises continues to refine its approach by exploring new markets across Kenya, forging deeper connections with diaspora clients, and introducing advanced digital solutions to simplify property acquisition.

  11. 2025

    Expanding Our Reach in Jikaze

    Famyard Enterprises further solidified its commitment to excellence by opening a second office in Jikaze. This strategic expansion increases accessibility, enhances client support, and reaffirms our role as a leader in modern, trustworthy land investments across Kenya.
