Overview

Welcome to Chaka Ranch Homes, a premium master-planned gated residential community strategically located next to the renowned Chaka Ranch Resort on the scenic foothills of Mount Kenya.

Designed for discerning homeowners and investors, this exclusive development offers fully serviced 1/4 acre plots within a secure and beautifully planned environment where luxury, nature, and long-term value converge.

Whether you're building your dream family residence, a serene holiday retreat, or securing a strategic investment in Nyeri’s rapidly growing premium property corridor, Chaka Ranch Homes offers unmatched potential.
